Privacy Policy

Momo ile Çocuklara Masallar · Last updated: May 30, 2026

Momo ile Çocuklara Masallar is a child-friendly story app. We do not collect, sell, rent, or share personal data for advertising or tracking.

Information We Do Not Collect

The app does not collect analytics, advertising identifiers, precise location, contacts, photos, microphone recordings, or browsing data.

Information Kept On Device

Child profile names, age group preferences, favorites, reading progress, app language, and notification settings may be stored locally on the device so the app can remember the family experience. This information is not sent to our servers.

Purchases

The app does not offer paid subscriptions or in-app purchases. All stories are available without payment.

Notifications

If a parent enables reminders, the app may schedule local bedtime or story recommendation notifications on the device. Notification settings can be changed in the app or in iOS Settings.

Children's Privacy

The app is designed for children and families. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe information has been provided to us by mistake, contact us and we will help resolve it.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact information-momo@protonmail.com.
