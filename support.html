Support

Momo ile Çocuklara Masallar · Last updated: May 30, 2026

Need help with stories, language, or notifications? Contact us at information-momo@protonmail.com.

Story Access

All stories in the app are available without paid subscriptions or in-app purchases.

Language

The app automatically uses Turkish for Turkey region devices and English elsewhere. Families can change the app language in the profile settings.

Notifications

Bedtime and story recommendation reminders are optional. They can be changed in the app profile settings or disabled from iOS Settings.

Response Time

We aim to respond to support emails within 2 business days.
